Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Your numbers stay on your phone

Everything you enter (your sex, bodyweight, lifts, records, events and settings) is saved in your browser on this device. It's never sent to us or anyone else, and there's no account.

  • To keep a copy or move to a new phone, use Back up under Your data on Progress. The backup is a file you keep; nothing is uploaded.
  • To remove everything, hold Clear my numbers under Your data, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Anonymous usage counts

To see whether the app is used and where it confuses people, it counts what happens, never what you type. For example: "reached the bench press screen", "finished the first visit", "logged a test", "opened Progress".

  • Counts go to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. Each count is the event's name only: no lifts, bodyweight, sex, class, dates or IDs.
  • GoatCounter sets no cookies and doesn't store your IP address. Like any web request, it sees your browser type and a rough location (country). See GoatCounter's privacy policy.
  • Nothing is counted if you switch off Share anonymous usage counts under Your data, or if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

The benchmarks

The rungs come from OpenPowerlifting's public-domain meet results: UK raw full-power results from 2024, as anonymous percentiles of groups of 20 or more adult lifters. No individual lifter's results are shown or stored in the app.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log requests (including IP addresses) to run its service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app loads nothing from any other site.
